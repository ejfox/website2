From 2016 to 2019 I was a senior interactive graphics specialist at NBC News, on the Foundry team. We built bespoke longform features, the [specials](https://www.nbcnews.com/specials), in collaboration with reporters across the newsroom. Every one was designed from scratch around its story, mixing video, data visualization and scrolling narrative.

![Scrolling Journey of a Bullet — the bullet-path diagram, documentary stills, and longform text as readers met it](https://res.cloudinary.com/ejf/video/upload/projects/nbcnews-specials/journey-scroll.mp4)

## Journey of a Bullet (2017)

The overlapping stories of six gunshot survivors, from Iraq to the streets of Chicago, coping after their injuries. [Read it →](https://www.nbcnews.com/specials/journey-of-a-bullet/)

![The introduction to the Journey of a Bullet story](https://res.cloudinary.com/ejf/image/upload/v1755100741/screenshot_2025-08-13_at_11.58.49_AM.png)

## Post Bail (2017)

America's justice system runs on exchanging money for freedom, so can data fix it? Jon Schuppe's investigation includes an interactive version of a risk-assessment algorithm, so readers can see how a score gets made. [Read it →](https://www.nbcnews.com/specials/bail-reform)

## Does America Need Another Prison? (2018)

Letcher County, Kentucky, was sustained by a coal industry that now hardly exists, and a federal prison might replace it. [Read it →](https://www.nbcnews.com/specials/kentucky-prison-coal-country)

![A map comparing the location of prisons and coal mines in the southeastern United States](https://res.cloudinary.com/ejf/image/upload/v1755100778/screenshot_2025-08-13_at_11.59.26_AM.png)

## How Trump Won (2016)

How a populist message proved everyone wrong, county by county. [Read it →](https://www.nbcnews.com/specials/donald-trump-republican-party/presidency/)
