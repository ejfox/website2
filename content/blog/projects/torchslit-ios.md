It's not actually a camera — it's a light-accompaniment: a strobe-light tool that scripts the iPhone's torch, meant to be paired with long-exposure photographs on my X-Pro3. A photographer's tool. It's still in development — I haven't really tried or tested it much yet; I've been fixing bugs with it.
