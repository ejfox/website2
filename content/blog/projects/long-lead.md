## Querdenken: COVID Misinformation or Freedom Movement?

Darren Loucaides' investigation into Germany's Querdenken movement and the 2020 storming of the Reichstag. I'm credited for production and technical support. The data graphics trace the audience growth of the Querdenken_711 and Qlobal Change Telegram channels (data: Telemetrio) and map the Telegram universe around Querdenken_711 (network data: Jordan Wildon / Logically).
