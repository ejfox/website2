A Twitter bot that takes news headlines about Syria and replaces the cities with American cities of similar size — Homs becomes Montgomery, AL; Richmond, VA stands in for a city under siege.

It's a lens-shift — seeing American place names described in the same way we do war-torn Middle Eastern cities is jarring and disorienting.

It didn't really get much response, but surfing the feed now is a really interesting historical artifact.

It strikes me as an interesting piece of internet art before I even knew what I was doing.
